# Literature Notes: Sheaf-Cohomological Obstruction

## Step 0 search results (session 2026-04-12)

Recent active directions:
  Lifting theorems: query complexity lower bounds lifted to communication complexity
    lower bounds, with improved lifting by Pang (2025). (source: Proof Complexity 2025, Oxford)
  Resolution over parities: connecting algebraic proof systems to circuit lower bounds
    for depth-2 circuits with parity gates. (source: Itsykson, Proof Complexity 2025)
  Monotone circuit complexity of matching: new lower bound techniques via monotone
    circuit arguments. (source: Cavalar, Proof Complexity 2025)
  New algebrization barriers: Chen, Hu, Ren (arXiv:2511.14038, Nov 2025) show PostBPE
    and BPE escape relativization but remain algebrization-blocked.
  Algebraic proof complexity: Tzameret (2025) state of the art survey; Elbaz proves
    lower bounds against Ideal Proof System in finite fields.

Recently refuted or critiqued directions:
  Monotone lower bounds: relativize, naturalize, and algebrize.
  GCT occurrence obstructions: Burgisser, Ikenmeyer, Panova (2019) proved these do
    not exist in the needed regime.

Unverified but notable recent claims:
  preprints.org/202601.2011: Multitime Barriers / TSM framework. Not peer-reviewed.
    No barrier evasion analysis provided.
